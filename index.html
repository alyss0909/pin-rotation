Set Up Your Six Months of Pins

Here is how to get your pins scheduling themselves for the next six months, all with free accounts. Five steps, start to finish.

Step 1

Make a GitHub account

This is where we will store your 70 pin images, instead of Google Drive, so each one has a real link that Pinterest can actually read. Already have a GitHub account? Skip the signup and just type your username below.

Open GitHub Signup

Step 2

Make your image folder

Name it pin-images and keep it set to Public, so Pinterest can see your images. Tick the box that says Add a README file, then click Create repository. That box gives your folder a starting point, which is what makes the upload step work.

Make the Folder

Step 3

Upload your 70 pins

Open the upload screen, drag all 70 images onto the big dotted box at once, wait for the list to finish loading, then click Commit changes at the bottom. Then open your folder and make sure all 70 are sitting there.

Open Upload ScreenOpen My Folder

Type your GitHub username in step one and these buttons light up.

Step 4

Make a Zernio account

This is what actually schedules and posts your pins for the next six months. Make the account, connect your Pinterest inside it, and copy your API key.

Open Zernio Open Keys Page

Step 5

Finish in your Pin Planner

On the Pin Images tab, click Fetch My Pin Images and type your GitHub username. Finish Batch Prep and Generate your pins. Then open The Pinterest Lounge, click Start My Rotation, paste your Zernio key, and Send. Your first week schedules while you watch, and the rest of the six months schedule on their own.

One rule for the next six months: do not rename or delete your pin-images folder. Your pins point to it.

Stuck on a step? Each one matches a numbered step in your 3.3 workbook.
