Set Up Your Six Months of Pins

Five steps, all free accounts. Then your pins schedule themselves.

Step 1: Make a GitHub account

This is where your 70 pin images live, so Pinterest gets a real link for each one. Already have one? Just type your username.

Open GitHub Signup

Step 2: Make a folder called pin-images

Keep it Public and tick Add a README file. Then Create repository.

Make the Folder

Step 3: Drag your 70 pins in

Drop them all onto the box at once, then click Commit changes at the bottom.

Open Upload ScreenOpen My Folder

Type your GitHub username in step one and these buttons light up.

Step 4: Make a Zernio account

This is what posts your pins. Connect your Pinterest inside it, then copy your API key.

Open Zernio Open Keys Page

Step 5: Back to your Pin Planner

Pin Images tab: click Fetch My Pin Images, type your username. Generate your pins like always. Then The Pinterest Lounge, Start My Rotation, paste your key, Send.

One rule: do not rename or delete your pin-images folder for six months. Your pins point to it.
