Set Up Your Six Months of Pins

Here is how to get your pins scheduling themselves for the next six months, all with free accounts. Seven steps, start to finish.

Step 1

Make a GitHub account

This is where we will store your 70 pin images, instead of Google Drive, so each one has a real link that Pinterest can actually read.

Open GitHub Signup

Step 2

Make your image folder

Name it pin-images, keep it set to Public, and click Create repository. Public is the setting that lets Pinterest see your images.

Make the Folder

Step 3

Upload your 70 pins

Enter your GitHub username, then open the upload screen. Drag all 70 images onto the big dotted box at once, wait for the list to finish loading, then click Commit changes at the bottom.

Open Upload Screen

Step 4

Check your folder

Make sure Pinterest can see all 70 images before you carry on. This checks your folder from the outside, the way Pinterest will.

Step 5

Copy your folder link

Copy the link to the whole folder, not a single image, and paste it into your Pin Planner.

Enter your GitHub username in step three

Step 6

Make a Zernio account

This is what actually schedules and posts your pins for the next six months. Make the account, connect your Pinterest inside it, and copy your API key.

Open Zernio Open Keys Page

Step 7

Finish in your Pin Planner

On the Pin Images tab, click Fetch My Pin Images and paste your folder link. Finish Batch Prep and Generate your pins. Then open The Pinterest Lounge, click Start My Rotation, paste your Zernio key, and Send. Your first week schedules while you watch, and the rest of the six months schedule on their own.

One rule for the next six months: do not rename or delete your pin-images folder. Your pins point to it.

Stuck on a step? Each one matches a numbered step in your 3.3 workbook.
